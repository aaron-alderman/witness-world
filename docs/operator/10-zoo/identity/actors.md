# Actors

## What it is

A first-class source of:

- agency
- authorship
- ownership
- authority
- stewardship

`actor` is not just a label on another object.

It is privileged in the authoring model and appears repeatedly as the initiating subject of authored change.

## Where it appears

RVM treats `actor` as a first-class form.

The broader authoring surface repeatedly carries:

- `actor`
- `owner`
- `steward`

Many authored objects and operations are interpreted through the current actor.

## What an author uses it for

- identify who is making a change
- identify who owns an object
- identify who may mutate an object
- identify who is being granted stewardship
- identify who a role grant or revoke applies to

## What it relates to

An actor participates in:

- stewardship
- proposals
- role grants and revokes
- app feature access policy
- process and witness authorship
- host/runtime identity

## Why it is special

Without `actor`, the system cannot answer:

- who did this
- who owns this
- who is allowed to do this
- whose perspective or authority is currently active

That makes `actor` a privileged primitive rather than an ordinary thing.
